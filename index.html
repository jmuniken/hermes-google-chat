Hermes Google Chat

A personal assistant that reviews messages in an explicitly approved Google Chat space and adds a Belgian-flag reaction to qualifying wins and positive team participation.

Operated by Jerome Muniken. This is a personal-use project, not an official Google or employer-provided service, and is not offered for public sign-ups.

How it works

The operator connects their own Google account using Google OAuth. A locally scheduled Hermes assistant reads authorized Chat messages, uses AI reasoning to assess them, and adds only the approved reaction. Existing reactions are checked to avoid duplicates.

Access and boundaries

The current automation is limited to one operator-approved space and the 🇧🇪 reaction. Message posting is disabled by local policy. Google permissions may be broader than the workflow; the privacy policya> explains access, AI processing, storage and revocation.

No Chat messages, attachments, account credentials or reaction logs are published on this website or in its public repository.
